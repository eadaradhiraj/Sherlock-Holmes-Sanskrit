Sherlock Holmes was a methodical thinker and his dress was neat, yet in his personal habits he had no orderliness. Tobacco in a Persian slipper, cigars in the coal-scuttle, unanswered letters pinned to the wooden mantelpiece with a knife, heaps of paper here and there, and chemical apparatus scattered everywhere. Tidying up was not in his nature, nor would he allow anyone else to tidy up. Like the king of idlers, day and night he sat in his armchair with either a violin or a book. One day, sitting right in his chair, he took a hundred cartridges and fired a pistol at the wall, chipping away the plaster with bullet marks to inscribe the letters 'V. R.'

Those documents were as dear to him as his own ribs. However, this happened only once. Afterwards, he was overcome by laziness once again. Sherlock's enthusiasm flares up, only to subside in the very next moment.
