Holmes - "In that moment his eyes fell on the documents kept on the table. "Then by the first train, he went to Woking. Examining the stolen goods, his idea was that they were truly valuable. Then he hid them in a safe place. He had planned that within a day or two he would sell them in a place like the French Embassy where a large sum of money could be obtained in exchange.
